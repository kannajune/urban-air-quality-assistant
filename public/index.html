How it works

  1. Read the air

    We pull the live US AQI, PM2.5, PM10, NO2 and ozone for your place, plus the next 24 hours.

  2. Know your household

    Children, older adults, asthma, your commute, your cooking fuel and your budget all change the advice.

  3. Get a plan with impact

    Google Gemma writes a routine, indoor steps and greener swaps, and explains what each one changes.

What the AQI number means

  • 0 to 50Good
  • 51 to 100Moderate
  • 101 to 150Unhealthy for sensitive groups
  • 151 to 200Unhealthy
  • 201 to 300Very unhealthy
  • 301 and aboveHazardous
